Am I a hobby tourist?

It's a term usually used with derision, a form of "no true Scotsman" or "name five of their albums" gatekeepy bullshit. And now in the classic "humor as self defense" mechanism, I'm possibly putting the label on myself.



<!--more-->

I've had a lot of hobbies. And I've abandoned a lot of hobbies. Every so often I come across something that seems interesting or fun and I think to myself, "maybe this is my *thing*."

It never is.

### like it's hot


As I've mentioned [before](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260702-guitar-hunt-pt1/) I've dabbled in music a lot. In addition to the guitars I've had, gotten rid of, and are looking for, I still have at least 2 midi keyboards lying around, 3 teenage engineering pocket operators, 3 cassette players, 2 micro casette players, a portablism sc500 mini dj scratcher, and a mini korg saved in a bookmark. I've done music basically through my whole life but it's been so sporatic, to borrow a term from *Clueless*, that I only just recently sat down and committed to learning or [finishing more than one song at a time](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260714-liars-work-ep/). 

I have a pair of HOTAS (technically HOSAS since they're VKB Gladiators) that I haven't touched in a while because Star Citizen ate my bindings and I haven't been assed to recreate them from scratch. I have a vr headset I went hard in the paint for Mechwarrior and haven't really gamed with since. I have 2 different retro raspberry pi systems, one running a full size, custom fight stick based arcade cabinet and the other running gen 3 to gen 5 game emulation with retro controllers. I even have a razer tartarus gaming keypad I got specifically for escape from duckov because I had one back in college and it was great for my carpal tunnel. I haven't played that game since Dec of '25. Hell, I even played competitive Spy Party for a while.

I got into Warmachine/Hordes/Malifaux. After probably less than 5 years I got out of it. (To be fair, the playerbase imploded.) I got into Magic. I fell out of Magic. I got back into Magic. Now I'm [forcefully trying to eject myself](https://makertube.net/w/rKZedep8Au3pejXgc5g7N9) from anything and everything Magic. I started 40k with necrons. They're in storage. Then bought Tyranids. Trying to [offload them](https://makertube.net/w/2vVpEw8ZFzSxJYbewKhDMb). Now I'm like 2000 points deep in Tau and have ... 6 models painted for them. At least my [love for Skaven](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260418-two-if-by-sea/#skaven-ikit-claw-kitbash) has never waivered. 

I used to be really into homebrewing beer. Had bottles, grain, a crusher, moved from extract brewing to brew in bag style. Got out of that when the kid was born. 

I used to do weekly tabletop RPGs and board games. It's just so hard to find the time and friends move away.

I've hosted/produced/been involved in at least 3 different podcasts and one youtube channel. Abandoned or dropped for various reasons. 

Between college and now I have purchased two different drawing tablets and I can still barely draw anything. 

Years ago I ran a blog. Much like this one, in fact. And on that blog it had some digital archive posts about second hand floppy disks. I remember buying my first box of ebay floppies at the height of the covid lockdowns. I still have assorted boxes of floppies in my office - I never "finished" that project.

I have at least two unfinished games I've started programming and a handful of other ideas I haven't even put to paper. 

For a couple years I built keyboards. Taught myself how to solder, put together kits and rigged up individiual parts and pcbs into functional (mostly) input devices. The oddball was probably my favorite, a split ortho keeb with a trackball built into the right thumb. I still have all my tools and a box of spare parts, but my daily driver is a retail durgod fusion anymore.

### in the yes column

The two things I can think that are my real "ongoing" hobbies anymore are photography and Warhammer/miniatures painting, and even then I burned myself out on photography. 

Not counting burnout, I've done photography "seriously" (as in, had a legit camera) since 2018 (almost ten years!). Since then I've collected a lot of gear. Four cameras (not including three I've inherited), easily a dozen lenses, and a bunch of assorted accessories like lighting and tripods.



### midwest emo

I started to create a list comparing "what hobbies I've abandoned" and "what hobbies I still do" but honestly it got too depressing. It's hard not to think about it in terms of "time wasted" and "money wasted." I know it's not that simple, but complex critiques are hard and scary.
