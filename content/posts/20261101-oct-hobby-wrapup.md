### so many rats, so little time

Amazingly this month wasn't completely rats. But like, 85% rats. I'm still playing Skaven-catch up for the escalation league which dictated most of my painting this month.

Let's see if I can remember to go in chronological-ish order.

<!--more-->

### a leader

The same day as the Globetrotters I finished the Clanrat Leader/Champion, which meant I had a completed brick of 20 painted Clanrats, seen below:

![rat](/images/octwrapup/clanrats1.jpg)

That means I'm on to painting my next brick of 20, but I'm going for a ~~faster~~ more stylized design for those Clanrats: based on that 5 color greyscale rat from [August](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260901-august-paint-wrapup/#and-the-chaff), this new block of 20 will be Eshin aligned and shadowy.

This poor quality photo shows you the general idea, using the same greyscale pallette with some topside edge highlighting in oranges and yellows to give them a torchlit feel.

![rat](/images/octwrapup/eshin.jpg)

I went back and torchlit the one from August, so I'm 3 clanrats deep into the new brick (these two pictures are the two that were painted in October).

### rootin shootin

The next elite unit I was supposed to have done by the end of October were Ratling Guns. Did I succeed? No. But I finished one gun:

![rat](/images/octwrapup/ratgun1.jpg)
![rat](/images/octwrapup/ratgun2.jpg)

For all the complaining I did about the Globetrotters, I think I dislike painting these even more. It's really a two-fold issue: for a 3-model unit, you're essentially painting 6 Clanrats. And beacuse they're both shoved on a calvary base, it's hard to reach between them. I could see these made easier by painting in sub or at least painting the two Skaven before attaching it to the base, but because they're attached there'd probably be some weird issues with painting in sub then attaching them correctly.

### and that's a bad miss

Back in the realm of Bloodied Bowls, The Quetza Atl Coatls got one step closer to fully-kitted with the completion of Vul-Kanno Buhr-Eeto.

![rat](/images/octwrapup/bb1.jpg)
![rat](/images/octwrapup/bb2.jpg)

For being in the home stretch of finishing this team, I am really dragging ass on painting these lizards. Aside from scheduling and time availability from trying to paint every goddamn Skaven I own for this league, I mainly blame all the golden baubbles on these models. There's so goddamn many. 

That and my early decision to wash everything on the lizards in a different color wash, which really makes the detail-portion of painting them take a long time. But I believe I'm down to my last Saurus and my last Skink.

### what's in your hobby

That's right, it's hobby time. First, the AoS escalation league game. Spoilers: I only got one in this month, the second one is happening *in November* (gasp!).

![rat](/images/octwrapup/score.jpg)

Being a 1000 point game, it gave me more freedom to a) take the things I like (fuck you grey seer) and 2) get away from the constraints of spearhead (fuck you grey seer).

My list was pretty simple, largely dictated by what I had painted:

Grey Seer on Screaming Bell (Scourge of Aqshy) [330 pts]
* Clanrats [150 pts]:

Arch-Warlock [140 pts]
* Acolyte Globadiers [90 pts]:
* Ratling Guns [160 pts]
* Warp Lightning Cannon [120 pts]

I definitely came out of the game with some better ideas with how to utilize what I have, since my ranged attackers are pretty squishy. (Plot twist: the answer is Gnawholes.)

I didn't actually get the use the Gnawholes the way I wanted because the first two turns I kept failing my Screaming Bell Pealing Portal cast so I couldn't just teleport a brick of Clanrats behind enemy lines. I did eventually get to do that, using the Clanrats to snag an Objective Pair but by that point I had pretty much tabled my opponent. 

The real MVP we made along the way was the Vermintide manifestiation, which held off a unit of cavalry plus a horse dad for two turns before just straight up getting unsummoned (it could not be killed).



![rat](/images/octwrapup/game1.jpg)

I also painted these little fire indicators, since the mission for the month involved terrain starting on fire. It happened to me, causing my Screaming Bell some mortal wounds, and it can happen to you.

![rat](/images/octwrapup/fire.jpg)

### leave it on the pitch

On the other end of games played, Round 1 of the Third Annual First Annual MacGuffin Cup came and went, and the Quetza Atl Coatls faced up against a Bretonnian team. Ultimately it was a pretty close game, losing 3 to 2 but the water lizards did manage to turn 1 throw down a casualty resulting in a crippling injury, continuing to cement them as the dirty players they are. On the same turn they suffered a Badly Hurt injury, so what goes around comes around I guess. 

I built the team around including the Krox off the bat, but that meant not having any Chameleon Skinks, which I found is really killing me re: passing (as in, the rest of the team just sucks at passing). I had at least 3 failed passes, mostly due to the thrower making inaccurate passes that went everywhere but near the catcher.

![rat](/images/octwrapup/bbscore1.png)

The close scoring game and the positive casualty infliction landed me right in the middle of the pack, standings wise going into Round 2. Leader of the loser bracket, some might say. I'm up against Tomb Kings next, so we'll see how I fare against something more punchy.
