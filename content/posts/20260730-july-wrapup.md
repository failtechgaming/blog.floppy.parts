### Can't have your puddin if you don't eat your meat

July has been the month of cleaning off my ~~plate~~ desk, and trying to finish half-finished projects. My painting desk is an absolute mess, partially due to these unfinished models strewn about as reminders to myself to finish them. It doesn't really work.

<!--more-->

### Hell of a hat that you're wearing

But first, I did finally and truly finish the Skablins by finishing the second Troll, Beatdown. 

![Troll](/images/julywrapup/troll1.jpg)
![Troll](/images/julywrapup/troll2.jpg)

This troll was a true kitbash, using the Blood Bowl troll has a base and modifying it with some AoS Fellwater Troggoth bits to replace both arms and to add a fin to the back instead of the spikes. Replacing the right hand/arm was the main priority, as I didn't want to paint yet another extra Goblin. The hat was also added, I suppose. I also re-arranged the shoulder pad to the other arm, and used a spikey-ball bit from *something* to cover the shoulder seam. 

The downside of all this bashing kits was it created a much more closed-off posture, making some of the painting more difficult. I liked the cell-shaded look that Agrax gave the base colors on the first troll I went with that again. No use fighting what looks good. 

So with the whole team finished, I could finally give them a little photo shoot and sizzle:

### Get on the floor

Another vegetable that I finished off my dinner plate was a Blood Bowl Saraus Blocker. That's right, Blood Bowl is back on the menu! The Lizard team is the team I played in the very first Blood Bowl season I took part in 3 or so years ago, but I still had 4 players to finish painting by the time the season was over (make that 3 now). I'm strongly thinking of playing them for the MacGuffin Cup Season 3, which gives me a push to get them finished. 

![Troll](/images/julywrapup/dino1.jpg)
![Troll](/images/julywrapup/dino2.jpg)

After painting the Skablins, it's crazy how many little bangles and dangles the Lizards have. Some might say they have *too many* details. I do immediately miss painting ska checkerboards though. 

Still on the Blood Bowl theme, I did also paint this fun little Goblin football. A quick paint, but I like having these on 25mm bases to use in the game to show a loose ball (and the only other one I have finished at the moment is a Lizard team snake-egg ball.) I didn't count it on my painting chart, and it honestly is creating a bit of an existential crisis when it comes to my paint recording - this thing is pretty small and inconsequential, so my gut feeling is don't count it. But I sat down and made the effort to paint it, so count it. But if I count it, the "price per model" system I've been using to log retail values gets all warped, so don't count it. You can see the issues I'm having. Not helping is the fact that I previously counted a score-tracker for the Goblin team. I may eventually go back and remove that, but for now I'm not going to make any retroactive changes.

All of that to say, here's a football stuffed with explosives.

### Survey says

That brings us to a now well-tread result of 5 things finished this month.

![Troll](/images/julywrapup/chart1.png)

One thing you will notice is after last month's dog statue, I retroactively created an "Other" category so I can still log those projects in the totals, and the Dune statue is another entry in that category. Ultimately this means the 6 month painting total was actually 42, not 41, which gives a year-end goal of 84 (I guess).

![Troll](/images/julywrapup/chart2.png)

Two entries each in Blood Bowl and Necromunda this month don't change the total project numbers much, though the $35 for the troll does bolster Blood Bowl over the $200 mark. 

![Troll](/images/julywrapup/chart3.png)

Once again that troll punches above its weight (above its STR 5?) to jump the monthly total retail value back up to the "monthly average" (trendlines added this time around).

Blood Bowl stays in the top spot of the finished models chart with its slow and steady additions month over month (there being only one month where there was no Blood Bowl to be seen (June)). So we'll see if I finish painting it by September.
