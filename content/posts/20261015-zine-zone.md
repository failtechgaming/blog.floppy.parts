About a year ago, I had the crazy idea of making my own Zine. This is the burning wreckage of that experience.

<!--more-->

Okay maybe it's not that dramatic. But I did dabble in zine making. Zining, if you will. At the time I attended a lot of house shows (band-wise) for photography and thought that would be the outlet I could use to get my zine into the world. I got about 80% completed and then completely dropped the ball. No zine, didn't even attend the house show in question.

I figured I'd start small - a 16 page count, a cover, and something I could ideally create with my home scanner and print anywhere and staple together. Lots of print/cut/tape/scan workflow.

I still think it'd be interesting to do, but it's definitely a backburner idea for now. Instead, here's the zine layout I had, just as it was abandoned, blank unfinished pages included (the original idea is those pages would be content from some friends but the project never got that far).
