# Whoa we're halfway there

And until like the last week of the month I hadn't gotten anything done at all.

<!--more-->

June is going to be my lowest "scoring" month of the year (so far), only completing three things.

### Agrocola

I spent a significant portion of the month working on [one specific project](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260624-june-artists-statement-war-rig/) that encompassed more than just the painting - working on the camera and lighting set up, filming, uploading the filming, editing, recording VO. All told, it was probably in the realm of 22 to 25 work-hours across 19 days. Which doesn't sound like a lot but that's squeezed into lunch breaks, late nights, and an hour or two here and there on the weekend. Or; all my "normal" painting time.

![Hot Wheels](/images/warrig/warrig1.jpg "zipties2")

Was it worth it? *Sure.* I've very happy with how it turned out, learned some new techniques, and had fun. Though I'm pretty sure having to paint with a light directly in my face in order to accomidate the overhead camera set up gave me neck pain I'm still recovering from. 

### Gin & juice

The next thing I finished isn't actually a table top miniature so recording it in **The Chart** is a little ambigious but I printed and painted a dog statue for my father as a belated Father's Day present (hi dad). 

I do wish I had some more time on this one, but was under the gun due to the holiday. I'd like to experiment with the white portions to give them more depth, but didn't want to "test in production" as I was trying to finish the model on time.

Not really much to this one. Painted to look like a dog. It looks like said dog. Dog dog dog.

![Dog](/images/junewrapup/dog1.jpg "Painted Dog")
![Dog](/images/junewrapup/dog2.jpg "Painted Dog")

### Year of the rat

The only other thing painted was a classic Kitchen Table Skaven. You know the kind. Your kid wants to paint miniatures, so you grab a Skaven Clanrat because if you're going to paint something in a poorly lit location with limited paints and brushes, a Clanrat can look like garbage and still be on theme. 

![Skaven](/images/junewrapup/skaven1.jpg "Skaven")
![Skaven](/images/junewrapup/skaven2.jpg "Skaven")

This Kitchen Table Skaven thing is also why I can never find all my Clanrats in one place. They're scattered to the winds across every surface in my house.
