# Mayday, m'aider

Another May day, another May dollar? Anyway, another month in the painting books. This time I did specifically clock my painting time from the beginning of the month, as mentioned [here](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260418-two-if-by-sea/#a-new-lap-record) but more on that later. 

### Pick it up x3

I finally managed to finish the starting lineup for the Skaville Skablins, completing the Bomma, Doom Diver, and Pogo back to back to back. I say starting lineup, because there's room on the roster for a second Troll, but a second troll was never part of the initial team build.

![Team](/images/maywrapup/wholeteam.jpg "The whole Skablins")

A second troll ~~has hit the tower~~ is being worked on but is not in a show-off state yet. The sizzle reel will wait until it's finished. Once that's done and the Skablins are good and truly finished, I'll probably go back around to finishing my Lizards, then move on to my other teams (Wood Elves for season 3??).

### τ

Moving on from Blood Bowl (you never truly can) I also finished two Tau Breachers. These things have been on my desk since like Q3 2025 and I'm still working on the 10 model squad. They're not *bad* to paint, they just keep being pushed down the priority list.

![Tau](/images/maywrapup/breacher1.jpg "Tau Breacher")

I'd show you a picture of the second one but it's the same as the first. 

As I work on these I'm still trying to perfect the pin washing line filler process. I've moved on from Tamiya Panel Liner for a couple of reasons, chief being I didn't care for the enamel base ruining my brushes and possibly my acrylic paintjob. Right now it's an ongoing experiment with contrast paint and water/contrast medium. One must imagine Sisyphus happy.

### 30 minutes or less

(And immediately we return to Blood Bowl.) Finally, the last thing painted was a real wildcard. Initially a test of some new FDM printer settings for miniatures, this little character soon became a "Biff the Understudy" type of fill-in as I tried to figure out how to make use of it in a game. To be fair, it was listed as a Blood Bowl proxy online, but it never stated what for. Anyway, I present to you, Mr. Pizzarino.

![Mr Pizzarino](/images/maywrapup/mrpizza.jpg "Mr Pizzarino")
![Mr Pizzarino](/images/maywrapup/mrpizza2.jpg "Mr Pizzarino")

As I don't yet have any proper Star Player models, Mr. Pizzarino will be filling in on the off-chance I'll be using one. He'll play any and all parts. And as it currently stands where I'll have 180,000 gp petty cash for my next game in the Blood Bowl league, he may be seeing some use, as a night goblin or a very aggressive squirrel.

### You run and you run to catch up with the sun but it's sinking

So as mentioned wayyy at the top, this month I took stock of how much *time* I spent painting and hobbying. Because stats are fun, or something. Since the app I use (Brushrage) is meant for commision projects (I believe), I categorized everything under a super-project for the month of May, and broke it out into further categories in order to keep painting separate from building, etc. 

![Per Month](/images/maywrapup/timerchart.jpg "Per Month Chart")

So ultimately, during the month of May I painted for just about twenty-one and a half hours. Slightly over half a standard work week. The majority of that time was painting (17hr59m or about 83% of total) as I really only built one model this month, the second BB Troll (which was largely a kitbash project). The "Other hobbying" in this case was mostly base rimming, grassing, and line painting on the BB model bases. I counted priming as painting for the sake of argument.

However, you might be asking yourself, "Why Coding, what is that *Contest - Devilfish* category? We haven't seen you talk about a Devilfish yet." Well, just like I talked about [last month](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260430-april-paint-wrapup/#youve-read-it-all-before) or technically even [before that](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260428-gloom-and-bloom/), I tried taking part in a local monthly painting contest. And like I mentioned [even further back](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260418-two-if-by-sea/#a-new-lap-record) I wanted to try logging my time on the contest model specifically. Beacuse again, stats are fun or something.

The long and the short of it (the average of it?) is I probably bit off more than I could chew with this contest selection. Not only did I select a Tau Devilfish, which, for the uninitiated, is a *whole ass vehicle* (and I've never painted a vehicle before), but this was also probably a bad month to attempt to do this. Our school system got out for the summer the third week of May, before Memorial Day, which means my painting time is/was significantly hampered and I pretty much lost a week of painting oppertunities. 

So I have to mark that one down as a DNF as far as the contest goes. I do still plan on finishing the model, probably within the next month, because I feel like I'm in the back half of it, progress-wise, and I know if I don't finish it now it will languish on my painting table forever and never get done. 

![Per Month](/images/maywrapup/timerstats.jpg "Per Month Chart")

The final piece of data is the stat breakdown for my time-keeping. Some of it isn't super helpful, such as *Total This Week*, as I don't really care on a week by week basis. But the number of days and monthly average are fun. The *paint sessions recorded* reinforces something I already knew - I tend to sit down and paint multiple times in a day instead of a single long session. In fact, on the 27 days I did paint, I averaged between 4 and 5 sitdowns. Sometimes more!

The Average This Month bit doesn't quite reflect that, as that's the total time (on average) for a day instead of a sit down - my sit down sessions are probably 5-15 minutes at a time, but I do it enough times a day to average about 45 minutes of painting a day. 

![Per Month](/images/maywrapup/WIP.png "Per Month Chart")

Okay I lied, this is the last last bit. The unfinished business I'm carrying over into the next month, like how Casper stuck around just to slowdance with a girl he knew for like a week or something. I keep saying I'll finish the Tau Commander, but I *totally, really will* finish the Devilfish. Then you'll see those 40k points jump all over the charts. *Then you'll see.*
