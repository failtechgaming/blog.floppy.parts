### Another day, another dollar, another ep

![EP cover](/images/disk1of1/SocialMediasquare.mp4.gif)

<!--more-->

That's largely a [lie](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260714-liars-work-ep/). But as I [have](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260702-guitar-hunt-pt1/) repeatedly [talked](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260709-guitar-pt-2/) about, since I have a [new guitar](https://blog.floppy.parts/posts/20260804-guitar-hunt-pt-3/) now it's been part of the creative process. While this EP still finds its basis on cassette sampling, splicing, and overdubbing, you might hear some Floppy Parts Originals guitar riffs in there. Each track contains at least one, if not more (except for the super secret bonus track, which was recorded while I was mastering the first EP, before the guitar was scored).
